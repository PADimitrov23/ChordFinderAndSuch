Strokes-style approach
  • Stay on the top three strings and keep the shapes tight, high up the neck.
  • Move between inversions of the same triad so the top note changes in small steps, almost like a melody.
  • With two guitars, split them up: one on root-position triads, the other on inversions in a higher spot, so the parts interlock instead of doubling each other.
  • Play them clean and a bit clipped (light palm-muting, tight downstrokes) rather than ringing out.
